01. Research Focus

Research Directions

Our lab focuses on fundamental and applied research at the intersection of AI, law, and information science.

R / 01

Legal AI & NLP

Deep learning for legal text analytics, document processing, and information extraction using state-of-the-art transformer models.

R / 02

Ethical & Responsible AI

Fairness, robustness, and explainability of AI systems in legal contexts. Addressing bias and ensuring trustworthy AI deployment.

R / 03

Legal Knowledge Representation

Developing knowledge graphs and semantic representations for legal information, enabling better reasoning and retrieval.

R / 04

Agentic AI & Automation

Building intelligent agents for legal document analysis, contract review, and regulatory compliance automation.

Key Research Areas

Legal Text Analytics Long Document Processing Information Extraction Case Law Retrieval Smart Contracts RegTech Solutions Legal Reasoning Low-resource Legal AI
